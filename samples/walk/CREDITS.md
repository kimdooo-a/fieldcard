## Voice notes (synthetic)

`voice-note-birds.mp3` and `voice-note-maples.mp3` are **synthesized speech**, generated with
ElevenLabs text-to-speech (premade voice "Will") from a script written for this sample, so the repo ships
nobody's real voice. Replace them with your own recordings from the phone's voice recorder.
